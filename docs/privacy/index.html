Privacy policy

Short version: we collect as little as possible, never sell it, and don't use ad trackers.

What we collect

  • Purchases: handled by Stripe. We see your name, email, country and what you bought, never your full card number.
  • Free starter + newsletter: if you sign up, we store your email and the page you signed up from, on Cloudflare R2, and send the Starter AGENTS.md through Resend. Your email is used only to send that file and The Drawer Notes. Reply "unsubscribe" or email us to be removed and deleted.
  • Support email: whatever you send to agentkitdrawer@agentmail.to.
  • Analytics: we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless and privacy-friendly. It counts page views, referrers, country and page load speed. It sets no cookies, uses no fingerprinting, and doesn't track you across sites.

Who processes data

Stripe (payments), Cloudflare (downloads, newsletter storage, DNS, analytics), Resend (sending the free starter email), GitHub Pages (hosting), fonts are self-hosted, so no third-party font requests.

Your rights

Ask us for a copy of your data or to delete it at agentkitdrawer@agentmail.to. We respond within 30 days.
