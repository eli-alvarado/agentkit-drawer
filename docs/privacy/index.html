Privacy policy

Short version: we collect as little as possible, never sell it, and don't use ad trackers.

What we collect

  • Purchases: handled by Stripe. We see your name, email, country and what you bought, never your full card number.
  • Newsletter: if you subscribe, we store your email and the page you signed up from, on Cloudflare R2. It's used only to send The Drawer Notes. Reply "unsubscribe" or email us to be removed and deleted.
  • Support email: whatever you send to agentkitdrawer@agentmail.to.
  • Analytics: privacy-friendly, cookie-free page-view stats (Cloudflare Web Analytics) if enabled. No personal profiles.

Who processes data

Stripe (payments), Cloudflare (downloads, newsletter storage, DNS, analytics), GitHub Pages (hosting), Google Fonts (fonts are loaded from Google's servers, which receive your IP address).

Your rights

Ask us for a copy of your data or to delete it at agentkitdrawer@agentmail.to. We respond within 30 days.
